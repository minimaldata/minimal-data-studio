Definitions and source data

MRR is monthly recurring software revenue, not cash collected. ARR is MRR × 12, not a revenue forecast. Net revenue retention compares recurring revenue from the same accounts 12 months apart; missing history displays —. Cash includes software, services, and usage payments.
